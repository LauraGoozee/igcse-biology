Osmosis in a living system (potato) and diffusion in a non-living system (agar cubes).

Osmosis in potato cylinders

Potato cells are living. Water moves by osmosis from a dilute solution to a more concentrated solution, across a partially permeable membrane.

Diffusion into agar cubes

Agar is non-living. The cubes contain an indicator that is pink in alkali. They are put in acid. As acid diffuses in, the cube turns colourless from the outside in.

Smaller cube → bigger surface area to volume ratio → acid reaches the centre faster. This is why cells are small, and why large organisms need exchange surfaces and transport systems.
