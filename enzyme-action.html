Key words

enzymea protein that is a biological catalyst: it speeds up reactions without being used up
substratethe molecule the enzyme acts on
active sitethe part of the enzyme where the substrate fits
complementarythe shapes fit together exactly, like a lock and key
enzyme–substrate complexthe enzyme with the substrate in its active site
productsthe new molecules made in the reaction

The example is amylase breaking down starch into maltose. Shapes are simplified.
