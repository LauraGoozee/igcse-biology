Enzyme Temperature Lab

How does temperature affect how fast amylase digests starch? Test at different temperatures, repeat, and find the optimum.

Amylase, starch and temperature

Amylase digests starch into maltose. Every 30 seconds, put one drop of the mixture into iodine on a spotting tile. When the iodine stays orange-brown, all the starch has gone.

blue-black = starch still there
