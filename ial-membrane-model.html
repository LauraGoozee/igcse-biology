Membrane permeability (model)

Why "fluid mosaic"? Fluid, because the phospholipids and many proteins move sideways within the bilayer. Mosaic, because proteins are scattered through the bilayer like tiles in a mosaic.

2

How the model was built from evidence

The fluid mosaic model is an interpretation of data. Each earlier model explained the evidence of its time, and was changed when new evidence did not fit.

1890s

Lipids in the membrane · Overton

EvidenceLipid-soluble substances entered cells much faster than water-soluble substances.
InterpretationThe cell surface contains lipid.
1917

A single layer · Langmuir

EvidencePhospholipids spread on water formed a layer one molecule thick, with heads in the water and tails pointing out of it.
InterpretationThe membrane was modelled as a single layer of phospholipids.
1925

A bilayer · Gorter and Grendel

EvidenceLipid extracted from red blood cells and spread on water as a single layer covered about twice the surface area of the cells.
InterpretationThe lipid forms a layer two molecules thick, with hydrophobic tails facing inwards.
1935

Protein sandwich · Davson and Danielli

EvidenceMembranes had a lower surface tension than pure lipid, and contained protein.
InterpretationA lipid bilayer coated on both sides by continuous layers of protein.
1950s

Electron microscope images · Robertson

EvidenceElectron micrographs showed membranes as two dark lines with a light band between them.
InterpretationSeemed to support the sandwich model, with dark lines read as protein layers.
1960s

Problems with the sandwich

EvidenceFreeze-fracture electron microscopy split membranes down the middle and revealed particles inside the bilayer. Membrane proteins were found to have hydrophobic regions.
InterpretationProteins are embedded in and span the bilayer, not only coating its surfaces.
1970

Proteins move · Frye and Edidin

EvidenceMouse and human cells were fused, with their membrane proteins labelled with different fluorescent markers. The labels mixed across the whole hybrid cell within about 40 minutes at 37 °C, but much more slowly at low temperature.
InterpretationMembrane proteins diffuse sideways, so the membrane is fluid.
1972

Fluid mosaic model · Singer and Nicolson

EvidenceBrought together all of the evidence above.
InterpretationA fluid phospholipid bilayer with proteins scattered through it, some on the surface and some spanning the membrane. Still refined today as new data arrive.
