Common misconception: water does not move "to dilute" a solution. Every water molecule moves randomly. There is a net movement because more free water molecules reach the membrane from the side with the higher water potential. Pure water has the highest water potential (0 kPa); adding solute makes ψ more negative, which means lower.

Find the isotonic point

Plot mean percentage change in mass against concentration. Where the line crosses zero there is no net movement of water, so the water potential of the solution equals the water potential of the potato cells.
