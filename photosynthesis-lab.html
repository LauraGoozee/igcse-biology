What to look for

Learn these signs first. Then use them to work out which mineral is missing in each jar.

SolutionWhy the plant needs itWhat the plant looks like
Complete (all minerals)Has everything it needsTall, strong stem, many large dark green leaves. Healthy growth.
No magnesium (Mg²⁺)Magnesium is needed to make chlorophyll. Less chlorophyll means less light is absorbed, so less photosynthesis.Yellow leaves between the veins (chlorosis). The veins stay green. Some growth, but less than the complete solution.
No nitrate (NO₃⁻)Nitrate is needed to make amino acids, which make proteins for growth.Stunted growth (short, thin stem). Small leaves. Older, lower leaves turn yellow.
Distilled water (no minerals)No minerals at allVery little growth. Very small, pale yellow leaves.
