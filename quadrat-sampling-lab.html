Quadrat Sampling Lab

Use random quadrats to estimate the population of daisies in two different areas.

Daisies in two areas

Each area is a 10 m × 10 m square of the school field. Area A is in the open. Area B is under trees. Use a 1 m × 1 m quadrat to estimate how many daisies there are.

Why random? Random coordinates stop you choosing spots with lots of daisies (or very few). This avoids bias, so the sample represents the whole area.

Results

Place 10 quadrats in each area.

Estimated population = mean number per quadrat × total area ÷ area of one quadrat
= mean × 100 m² ÷ 1 m²
