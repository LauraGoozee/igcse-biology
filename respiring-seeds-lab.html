Respiring Seeds Lab

Show that living seeds give out carbon dioxide and heat when they respire.

Carbon dioxide from respiring seeds

Hydrogencarbonate indicator changes colour with the amount of carbon dioxide. Living, germinating seeds respire and give out carbon dioxide.

purple = less CO₂ than air
red = same as air
orange
yellow = more CO₂ than air

Why each tube? Tube B (boiled seeds) shows the CO₂ comes from living seeds. Tube C (glass beads) is a control with the same volume, so the change is not caused by the tube or the air. The gauze stops seeds touching the indicator.

Heat from respiring seeds

Respiration releases energy. Some is lost as heat. Vacuum flasks stop heat escaping, so we can measure it.

Day 0

Why? Boiled seeds are dead, so flask B is the control. Disinfectant kills microorganisms on the seeds. If they are not killed, the microorganisms respire and make heat too. The cotton wool plug lets oxygen in.
