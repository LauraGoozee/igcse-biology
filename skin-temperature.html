    Exam answer · too hot
    1. Vasodilation: arterioles supplying the skin capillaries widen.
    2. More blood flows near the skin surface.
    3. More heat is lost by radiation.
    4. Sweat glands release more sweat.
    5. Sweat evaporates, which cools the skin.
    Exam answer · too cold
    1. Vasoconstriction: arterioles supplying the skin capillaries narrow.
    2. Less blood flows near the skin surface.
    3. Less heat is lost by radiation.
    4. Less sweat is produced, so less heat is lost by evaporation.
    5. Shivering: muscles contract quickly; respiration releases heat.
    Avoid these mistakes
    • Capillaries do not move up or down in the skin.
    • Say the arterioles widen or narrow, not the capillaries.
    • Sweat cools you when it evaporates. It is not "cold sweat".
    • Vasoconstriction does not make heat. It reduces heat loss.
    • You do not need to write about hairs.
