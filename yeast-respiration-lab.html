Yeast Respiration Lab

Investigate anaerobic respiration in yeast: how do temperature and glucose concentration change the rate?

Yeast respiring without oxygen

Yeast is mixed with glucose solution. A layer of oil stops oxygen getting in, so the yeast respires anaerobically: glucose → ethanol + carbon dioxide. Count the bubbles of carbon dioxide.

What will you change? (independent variable)
Water bath temperature
Glucose concentration
Count: –

Why boil the glucose solution first, then cool it? Boiling removes dissolved oxygen. It must cool before adding yeast, or the high temperature would kill the yeast.
